# Third-party material

- **Brand logos in the Store and Connectors** come from [simple-icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0 1.0), bundled by `scripts/gen-brand-icons.mjs` into `apps/console/src/data/brandIcons.ts`. They are trademarks of their owners and appear only to identify the product they belong to. Brands simple-icons doesn't carry (for example OpenAI and Slack) show a monogram instead. The console never loads a logo from another site.
- **Adapter names** in the Store mirror the built-in adapter types of [Paperclip](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip). Its licence terms will be checked and its attribution kept before the fork lands.
